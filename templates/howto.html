How to Use Summer.GG

Your guide to levelling up this summer

Guide
The core loop — 3 steps
💪
1. Do stuff
Sports, reading, chores, finance lessons — anything on your schedule
⭐
2. Log XP
Hit the "Log" button on the Today tab after each activity to bank your points
🎮
3. Earn gaming
Hit the daily XP target and unlock your gaming hours window
⭐ How XP works

Every activity earns XP. Stack enough XP and you level up. Levels are just bragging rights — the real prize is the gaming window you unlock each day.

Activity XP Category
Taekwondo practice +100 Sports
Sports / movement +100 Sports
Reading (30 min) +100 Education
Finance lesson +150 Finance
Paper trade +75 Finance
Chores (full list) +500 Household 🔥
Family time (1 hr) +200 Family
Training session +200 Sports
Daily target: 500 XP
Hit 500 XP in a day and you unlock your gaming hours. Miss it and gaming is off. Your parent sets the exact cap (default: 4 hours).
📅 Today tab — your daily routine
🤖
AI builds your schedule
When the app starts, the AI coach looks at your recent XP and balance and generates a personalised schedule. You can also hit AI to regenerate or Manual to build your own.
✅
Log activities as you go
After finishing an activity, find it in today's schedule and tap Log +XP. The XP goes straight to your daily total and you'll see confetti 🎉.
🔥
Build your streak
Log at least one activity every day to keep your streak alive. The flame badge in the top bar shows your current streak days.
💰 Finance tab — learn money & invest

The Finance tab has two things: a learning track with real lessons about money, and a paper trading sandbox where you can buy/sell stocks with fake money.

📚
3 tiers of lessons — unlock in order
Tier 1 (green) — Financial literacy basics. Start here.
Tier 2 (purple) — Stock market and investing. Unlocks after Tier 1.
Tier 3 (locked) — Advanced strategy. Unlocks after Tier 2.
📝
How to complete a lesson
Click on a lesson to expand it → read the paragraph → answer all 3 quiz questions correctly → hit Claim 150 XP. The next lesson only unlocks after you claim XP on the current one. Got 2 wrong answers? The correct ones are revealed and you can still claim.
📈
Paper trading — no real money
Use the trade form to buy and sell stocks with fake money. Type your rationale and the AI coach will review your trade idea before you execute it.
Tip: use "Ask the finance coach"
There's an AI chat box on the Finance tab. Type any concept — "what is a P/E ratio?", "explain ETFs", "what is short selling?" — and get an instant plain-English explanation.
🏆 Streaks, levels & rewards
🔥 Streaks
Log at least one activity every day. The longer the streak, the more momentum you build. It shows in the flame badge at the top.
⚡ Levels
XP accumulates and pushes you through levels. The progress bar on the Today tab shows how far you are to the next level.
💵 Real earnings
Your parent can set a conversion rate (e.g. 500 XP = $1). Every XP you earn is tracked on the Parent tab as real money you've earned.
🏅 Rewards tab
Check the Rewards tab to see your progress towards milestones — it tracks what you've unlocked across the summer.
⚡ Quick tips
  • Chores are worth 500 XP — the biggest single earner. Do them early.
  • Finance lessons stack XP and teach you real-world skills. Don't skip them.
  • The AI coach note at the bottom of Today shows where your balance is weak — pay attention to it.
  • You can switch between dark and light mode using the sun/moon button in the top right.
  • Gaming is a reward, not a default. Earn it every day.
For parents — setup & controls
🔐 PIN & access
The Parent tab is PIN-protected (default: 1234 — change it immediately). From there you can override gaming caps, set the weekend XP minimum, and view the AI weekly summary.
💰 XP → real earnings
In Override Controls, set the XP → dollar rate (default: 500 XP = $1). The Real Earnings Tracker card on the Parent tab shows the total dollar equivalent earned over the summer. Pay it out however you prefer — weekly, monthly, or at the end of summer.
📚 Setting up finance lessons
25 lessons come pre-loaded. On the Parent tab → Generate AI Finance Lessons:

Step 1 — Pick a tier and click "Generate (up to 5)" to add new lesson titles via Ollama.
Step 2 — Click "Pre-generate all lesson content" to cache content for every lesson upfront. Without Step 2, each lesson generates on first expand (takes 10–30 seconds). With Step 2 done, every lesson opens instantly.
🔄 Resetting for a new summer
Parent tab → scroll to the bottom → Initialize / Reset App. Enter your PIN to confirm. This wipes all XP, logs, trades, and lesson progress but keeps your settings, activities, and lesson content.
⚙️ Customisation
In Override Controls you can:
• Set the child's name, age, and gender so AI prompts are personalised
• Adjust XP rates per activity to match your household priorities
• Set the daily gaming cap (max hours even if target is hit)
• Set the weekend XP minimum (lighter than weekdays but still required)
